Review the recent completed experiment records, failed records, decisions, external
receipts, checkpoints, and user corrections for this project. Resolve evidence by
attempt time, source revision, and immutable run or external ID; do not attribute

- the observed result and exact evidence link;
- the affected task, split, metric, configuration, and exposure conditions;
- plausible alternative explanations and contrary evidence;
- the bounded next action actually justified by the result;
- what result would reverse or narrow the conclusion;
- whether the evidence is development, exposed/adaptive confirmation, untouched

rejected results. Do not infer causation from a bundled change, turn a diagnostic
category or estimated opportunity into fact, generalize from repeatedly reused
origins, or claim that retrieval proves application. If a material failure was
fixed, verify that its record links the original signature, explicit root-cause
hypothesis, minimal patch, and targeted before/after regression check; otherwise
leave resolution unverified.

Propose at most three useful lessons with narrow applicability and specific
supporting run/file references through godel_propose_lesson, then save a checkpoint
containing the acceptance status, consolidated remaining budget, closure state,
and next action. Tell the user how to inspect proposals with /lessons and /accept.

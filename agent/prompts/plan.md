Read the project brief and checkpoint. Identify the desired deliverable, data,
validation design, baseline and budget. Ask focused questions about consequential
gaps, while continuing independent inspection. Propose the next small useful
step, update the brief with confirmed decisions, and save a checkpoint. Establish
protocol.md: prediction-time assumptions, split rationale, diagnostics, candidate
budget and capacity for confirmation. If resuming, account for prior searches
and address promised validation work before proposing more configurations.
State the next falsifiable question, cheapest useful test, acceptance constraints
and stopping condition. Establish a baseline quickly instead of extending planning.
